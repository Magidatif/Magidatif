## 🏥 Featured Project: Claims Denial Analysis & Financial Impact Dashboard

### Turning Healthcare Data into Actionable Insights

In healthcare, we often focus on clinical outcomes, but the **financial health** of a hospital is what ensures we can continue providing high-quality care to every patient.

Coming from a background as a **Pharmacist**, I've always believed that a precise diagnosis is the first step toward a cure. Today, I apply that same philosophy to hospital operations by using data to **"diagnose" and "treat" revenue leakage.

I recently developed the **Claims Denial Analysis & Financial Impact Dashboard** to provide a transparent look into the Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) process. This isn't just about charts; it's about understanding the **"why"** behind the numbers:

- 🔍 **Identifying the Root Cause:** By analyzing **288,625 claims**, I identified that reasons like *"Coverage not found"* (MB-NC-01) represent the largest financial burden — totaling over **13M EGP**. This insight allows us to fix issues at the front-end registration before they become costly errors.

- 💰 **Understanding the Revenue Gap:** A **65.4% Revenue Gap Rate** is a significant call to action. Every recovered claim is a resource that can be reinvested into better medical equipment, staff training, and patient facilities.

- 👨‍⚕️ **Empowering Clinicians:** By tracking performance down to the provider level, we can offer targeted support and training, reducing the administrative burden on our medical teams so they can focus on what they do best: saving lives.
